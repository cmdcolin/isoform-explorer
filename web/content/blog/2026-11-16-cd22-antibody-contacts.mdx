CD22 is a receptor on B cells, and
[Ereño-Orbea et al. (2017)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-017-00836-6) write
that "as a B-cell-restricted antigen, CD22 is targeted in therapies against
dysregulated B cells that cause autoimmune diseases and blood cancers". Its
extracellular part is a row of seven Ig-like domains, which the structural
papers number d1, at the tip, to d7, next to the membrane. UniProt names d1
Ig-like V-type ({d(1)}) and d2 to d7 Ig-like C2-type 1 to 6, the names the gene
page uses. Of the {record.isoforms.length} alternative isoforms UniProt
describes, CD22-alpha lacks residues 241–417, which take in d3 ({d(3)}) and d4
({d(4)}) whole:

> {s.answer}

When isoforms of a protein lack whole domains, an antibody against the protein
may have its contact residues in some isoforms and not in others. For
an antibody whose complex with CD22 has been crystallised, those residues can be
counted in each isoform, and this post counts them for two antibodies.

## Two crystal structures

- **m971, PDB [7O52](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/7O52).** The Fab of m971
  in complex with CD22 d6–d7, at {m971.resolution} Å
  ([Ereño-Orbea et al., 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbc.2021.100966)).
  According to that paper, T cells with chimeric antigen receptors derived from
  m971 "have shown promise when used as an immunotherapeutic agent against
  B-cell acute lymphoblastic leukemia", and the m971 epitope is on d7.
- **Epratuzumab, PDB [5VL3](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/5VL3).** The Fab of
  the therapeutic antibody epratuzumab in complex with CD22 d1–d3, at{' '}
  {epratuzumab.resolution} Å
  ([Ereño-Orbea et al., 2017](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-017-00836-6)). The
  asymmetric unit holds {epratuzumab.copies.length} copies of the complex.

## How the residues are counted

A contact residue here is a CD22 residue with any protein atom within 5 Å of a
protein atom of the Fab. Residues are numbered on UniProt's canonical sequence
through RCSB's alignment of each structure to it. In 5VL3, each CD22 copy is
paired with the Fab it has the most residues within 5 Å of, and contacts
with a neighbouring copy's Fab, which come from how the complexes pack in the
crystal, are left out. The four copies have{' '}
{Math.min(...epratuzumab.sizes)}–{Math.max(...epratuzumab.sizes)} contact
residues each; this post counts the {epratuzumab.contacts.length} residues that
all four have ({epratuzumab.any} appear in at least one).

Each contact residue is then present or missing in each isoform, from the same
UniProt edits the gene page draws. An isoform that differs from the canonical
only outside the stretch a structure covers cannot be assessed from that
structure, and the table marks it.

A crystal contact set is not the epitope: these are residues within 5 Å of the
antibody in one structure. That an isoform keeps them says nothing about whether
it folds the same way or binds.

## m971 and epratuzumab across the isoforms

All {m971.contacts.length} m971 contact residues are in every isoform, but each
of the four isoforms differs from the canonical only outside the stretch 7O52
covers ({span(...m971Span)}), so none of their edits falls within this
structure.

The {epratuzumab.contacts.length} epratuzumab contact residues are{' '}
{positionsText(epratuzumab.contacts)}, in d2 and d3 except for{' '}
{positionsText(between)}, between the two domains. CD22-alpha has {alpha.present} of
them, and the {alpha.missing.length} it lacks, residues{' '}
{positionsText(alpha.missing)}, are in d3.

Isoform 5 lacks residues {absentText(five)}, which include all but the first
residue of the signal peptide, d1 and the start of d2. It has {fiveStates.present} of the {fiveStates.total} epratuzumab contact
residues and lacks {positionsText(fiveStates.missing)}; of those present,{' '}
{fiveStates.abutting.length === 1 ? 'one' : fiveStates.abutting.length}, residue{' '}
{positionsText(fiveStates.abutting)}, is within {ABUT} residues of the missing
stretch. Isoforms 3 and 4 differ from the canonical only outside the stretch
5VL3 covers ({span(...eSpan)}).

## Sugars and expression

The counts above leave out the sugars on CD22. The 2017 paper reports "a
critical role for CD22 N-linked glycosylation in antibody engagement", and its
abstract does not say which sugar. In 5VL3, sugars are modelled on asparagines{' '}
{positionsText(epratuzumab.sugarSites)} of every copy, and the sugar on{' '}
{positionsText(epratuzumab.sugarContacts)} has atoms within 5 Å of the Fab in
all four.{' '}
