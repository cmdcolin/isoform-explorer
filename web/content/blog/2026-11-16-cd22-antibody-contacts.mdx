CD22 is a B-cell receptor targeted in therapies for B-cell cancers and
autoimmune disease
([Ereño-Orbea et al., 2017](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-017-00836-6)). Its
extracellular part is seven Ig-like domains, d1 at the tip to d7 by the
membrane. UniProt names d1 Ig-like V-type ({d(1)}) and d2 to d7 Ig-like C2-type
1 to 6. Of the {record.isoforms.length} alternative isoforms, CD22-alpha lacks
residues 241–417, all of d3 ({d(3)}) and d4 ({d(4)}):

> {s.answer}

Where an antibody's complex with CD22 has been crystallised, its contact
residues can be counted in each isoform, as this post does for two
antibodies.

## Two crystal structures

- **m971, PDB [7O52](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/7O52).** Fab with CD22
  d6–d7, {m971.resolution} Å
  ([Ereño-Orbea et al., 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbc.2021.100966)).
  CAR T cells derived from m971 "have shown promise" against B-cell acute
  lymphoblastic leukemia, and its epitope is on d7.
- **Epratuzumab, PDB [5VL3](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/5VL3).** Fab with
  CD22 d1–d3, {epratuzumab.resolution} Å, {epratuzumab.copies.length} copies of
  the complex per asymmetric unit.

## How the residues are counted

A contact residue is a CD22 residue with a protein atom within 5 Å of the Fab,
numbered on UniProt's canonical sequence through RCSB's alignment. In 5VL3 each
copy is paired with the Fab it has the most residues within 5 Å of, so
crystal-packing contacts are left out, and the post counts the {epratuzumab.contacts.length} residues all
four copies share ({epratuzumab.any} appear in at least one). Each is then
present or missing in each isoform, from the same UniProt edits the gene page
draws. An isoform that differs from the canonical only outside a structure's
stretch cannot be assessed from it, and the table marks it.

<Html html={contactTable(record)} />

Residues within 5 Å in one crystal are not the epitope, and an isoform keeping
them says nothing about folding or binding.

## m971 and epratuzumab across the isoforms

All {m971.contacts.length} m971 contact residues are in every isoform, and
every isoform's edits fall outside the stretch 7O52 covers
({span(...m971Span)}).

The {epratuzumab.contacts.length} epratuzumab contact residues,{' '}
{positionsText(epratuzumab.contacts)}, are in d2 and d3 except{' '}
{positionsText(between)}, between them. CD22-alpha has {alpha.present} and lacks{' '}
{positionsText(alpha.missing)}, all in d3.

Isoform 5 lacks residues {absentText(five)}, which include all but the first
residue of the signal peptide,
d1 and the start of d2. It has {fiveStates.present} of {fiveStates.total} and
lacks {positionsText(fiveStates.missing)}; of those present,{' '}
{fiveStates.abutting.length === 1 ? 'one' : fiveStates.abutting.length},{' '}
{positionsText(fiveStates.abutting)}, lies within {ABUT} residues of the missing
stretch. Isoforms 3 and 4 differ from the canonical only outside{' '}
{span(...eSpan)}.

## Sugars

N-linked glycosylation in antibody engagement". In 5VL3, sugars sit on

on {positionsText(epratuzumab.sugarContacts)} touches the Fab in all four.{' '}
