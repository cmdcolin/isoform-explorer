PAR3, encoded by _F2RL2_, is a protease-activated receptor. Thrombin cuts its
tail outside the cell between residues 38 and 39, and UniProt annotates residues
22–38 as a propeptide removed for receptor activation. Ahead of the propeptide,
residues 1–21 are a signal peptide.

The drawing above lays PAR3 across the membrane from UniProt's topology
features, once for the canonical protein and once for isoform 2. The gene page
sums up isoform {s.isoform.accession} in one sentence:

> {s.answer}

## Where isoform 2 starts

_F2RL2_ has two exons, and the canonical start codon lies near the end of the
first. Isoform 2's transcript ({s.isoform.transcripts.ensembl.join(', ')},
{s.isoform.transcripts.refseq.join(', ')}) leaves exon 1 at an earlier splice
site, upstream of that start codon, so its exon 1 is untranslated. The first
start codon in the transcript is then the one for methionine 23, two bases into
exon 2, and the protein runs from there to the canonical end:{' '}
{s.isoform.length} residues against the canonical's {s.record.length}.

Isoform 2 keeps everything after residue 22: the rest of the propeptide, the
thrombin site and all seven transmembrane helices.

## How often the shorter first exon is used

No paper we found studies isoform 2. Its RefSeq transcript comes from a cDNA of
mesangial cells ([AK298585.1](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/nuccore/AK298585.1)),
and no peptide unique to it has been reported.

GTEx v8's transcript TPM gives {s.isoform.transcripts.ensembl[0]} a median of 0
in every tissue. The junction from the isoform's earlier splice site into exon 2
is there all the same: it has {share(e)} of the reads into exon 2 across GTEx,
from {tissueShare(e, 'Heart - Left Ventricle')} in heart left ventricle to{' '}
{tissueShare(e, 'Nerve - Tibial')} in tibial nerve, with no tissue standing out.
