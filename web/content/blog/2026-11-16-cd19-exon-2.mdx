[Sotillo et al. (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1158/2159-8290.CD-15-1020) found
CD19 mRNA "lacking exon 2" in leukaemia that relapsed after CAR T therapy.
Exon 2 skipping "allows expression of the N-terminally truncated CD19 variant,
which fails to trigger killing by CART-19".
