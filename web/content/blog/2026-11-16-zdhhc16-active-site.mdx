ZDHHC16 is one of the human palmitoyltransferases named for their DHHC motif
(aspartate, histidine, histidine, cysteine). These enzymes attach a palmitate to
the motif's own cysteine first and pass it on to a substrate second
([Rana et al., 2018](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aao6326)). UniProt
annotates the DHHC domain at residues 155–205 and marks the motif's cysteine,
C185, as the active site, an S-palmitoyl cysteine intermediate. The drawing
above shows the palmitate on C185. The gene page sums up isoform{' '}
{s.isoform.accession} in one sentence:

> {s.answer}

The removed stretch, residues 176–214, includes the whole motif, D182 to C185.

## Two splice changes, 117 bases

The gene page draws isoform 3 with GENCODE transcript{' '}
{s.record.structures[s.isoform.accession]?.transcript}. Against the canonical
transcript, it skips exon 6, which is 29 bases, and starts exon 7 88 bases late.
Together that is 117 bases, or 39 codons, so the frame holds and the protein
continues from residue 215 to the canonical end. UniProt maps isoform 3 to{' '}
{s.isoform.transcripts.ensembl[0]} and{' '}
{s.isoform.transcripts.refseq.join(', ')}.

Isoform 3 also lacks the first 16 of the 21 residues of the fourth transmembrane
helix (199–219).

## Where isoform 3 is made

No paper we found studies isoform 3. Its sequence comes from a cDNA of tonsil B
cells ([BC033157.1](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/nuccore/BC033157.1)), and no
peptide unique to it has been reported by mass spectrometry, so the evidence is
RNA only, and there is plenty of it. In our tally of GTEx v8 junction reads (see
[the expression post](../2026-11-16-where-isoforms-are-made/) for the method),
the isoform's junction, which skips exon 6 and enters exon 7 late, is in{' '}
{e.isoformSamples.toLocaleString()} of {e.samples.toLocaleString()} samples and
has {share(e)} of the reads at its splice site overall. The share varies by
tissue:

- {tissueShare(e, 'Muscle - Skeletal')} in skeletal muscle
- {tissueShare(e, 'Heart - Left Ventricle')} in heart left ventricle
- {tissueShare(e, 'Brain - Frontal Cortex (BA9)')} in frontal cortex
- {tissueShare(e, 'Cells - Cultured fibroblasts')} in cultured fibroblasts and{' '}
  {tissueShare(e, 'Esophagus - Mucosa')} in esophageal mucosa

GTEx's transcript TPM for {s.isoform.transcripts.ensembl[0]} gives a similar
pattern. Mice lacking ZDHHC16 develop cardiomyopathy
([Zhou et al., 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4697436/)), a
finding about the gene. We found no study of what the isoform without the
catalytic cysteine does in muscle or heart.
